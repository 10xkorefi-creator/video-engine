AI ACCOUNTANT (aiaccountant.com) — facts the script may use (source: aiaccountant.com homepage and Tally integration guide)
- Tally-native: works ON TOP of Tally (TallyPrime and Tally.ERP 9). You keep Tally; AI Accountant automates the work around it. No migration needed.
- Bank statements: upload them, AI reads every transaction, categorises it, maps it to the right ledger, and syncs to Tally in one click. No manual entry.
- GST / ITC: upload Purchase Register and GSTR-2B; AI does the matching, flags mismatches, and fills Table 4 of GSTR-3B for accurate ITC claims.
- Vendor bill matching and bookkeeping automation.
- Real-time dashboards from Tally data: cash flow, receivables, payables, runway, without spreadsheets.
- Claim on the site: "Get rid of 75% of your accounting tasks."
- ISO certified, SOC 2 Type II certified, 300M+ transactions analysed.
- Setup typically done in hours; supports multi-company, inventory and branch reporting.
- Also offers services: virtual accounting, payroll & TDS, ROC compliance, tax compliance.
- Free tools: bank statement converter (PDF to Excel/CSV), GST rate finder, invoice generator.

POSITIONING
- Story to tell: "Don't switch software, upgrade how you use Tally." Tally is the books; AI Accountant removes the manual work (data entry, reconciliation, GST matching) on top of it.
- Zoho Books and other software may be named factually as alternatives (e.g. "switching to Zoho Books means migrating everything"), never mocked, and with no claims about them that the sources do not support.
