# Reel script template

Every reel script follows this template. It is built from our video producer's best-performing scripts
("Where is my 6L?", "Sharma Ji", "Who can read this?", the UGC scripts). The script writer is given it
word for word (`src/script.js`), and drafts that break it are rejected and rewritten (`validateShape`).

Target: 40–50 seconds, 90–110 spoken words, 11–15 beats.

## 1. HOOK (1–2 beats, first ~4 s)

Drop the viewer into the problem. The hook itself carries the tension.

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Mini-drama, mid-scene | "Where did 6 lakh go?! Who entered this bill?" |
| Play-along challenge (top converter) | "What's your cash flow for last quarter? Answer in 5, 4, 3... time's up." |
| Role callout + provocation | "Accounting bros, listen to me! Why are you still opening dashboards?" |
| Specific stake | "One duplicate invoice. One extra payment. How would you catch it?" |
| Contrarian flex | "I haven't opened my Tally in 3 weeks." |

An optional second beat escalates the same moment. It never explains.

Banned: "stay till the end", "in this video", "let me tell you", "did you know", greetings, or a flat
report of what someone did with no tension.

Self-test: if this were the only line people heard, would they need to know what happens next?

## 2. BRIDGE (exactly 1 beat, by ~5–8 s)

One short rhetorical question (max 12 words, ends with "?") that says the absurd or painful truth
out loud. On screen: freeze-frame, record scratch, and a 2–5 word text punch.

> "So my accountant has to be a handwriting expert now?"

## 3. BODY (6–10 beats)

The answer to the bridge: the one idea from the blog, told through the hook's characters
(Rohan the founder, Sharma ji the accountant, Priya the young CA).

1. The turn: "Or... what if a photo could read it for you?"
2. 3–6 beats of how it works or why it matters, one concrete step or consequence each. Worked figures go on the paper canvas.
3. One re-hook halfway, true to what follows: "And it gets worse."
4. The payoff: the one habit, rule or fix, with AI Accountant only when it directly solves the problem.

## 4. CTA (exactly 2 beats)

1. A callback punchline that closes the hook's loop: "Don't expect your accountant to be a handwriting expert. He's got a life too."
2. "Full breakdown on AI Accountant dot com."

## Delivery marks (for the voice)

- Every beat starts with an emotion tag, and the emotion changes across the reel: hook `[shocked]`/`[frustrated]`, bridge `[sarcastic]`, body `[serious]` then `[confident]`, punchline `[playful]`.
- Pauses: `...` before a reveal, `—` for a sudden turn, up to 3 `[pause]` tags.
- One key word in CAPITALS in at least 4 beats ("Not a SINGLE entry matched.").
- Mix 2–3 word punches with full lines.

The voice is ElevenLabs v3 in Creative mode (most expressive). If a read drifts from the script, it is
re-read in Natural mode.
